# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

This site exists solely to provide homepage and privacy-policy information for the HK-HorseRacing Google OAuth client.

## Data handling

- The OAuth client is used by rclone running on a user-controlled private virtual machine.
- Google Drive access is limited to reading the race-fixture CSV selected by the owner and uploading prediction PDFs to the owner's Google Drive.
- This website does not collect, store, sell, share, or publish personal information, OAuth tokens, Google Drive files, race data, prediction results, or VM connection details.
- Google OAuth credentials and tokens remain in the owner's local rclone configuration and are never included in this repository.

For questions about this OAuth client, contact the account owner through the GitHub account that maintains this repository.
